Audit summary

Weekday offset distribution

Signed offsets describe how recorded weekdays differ from the GCF result. Repeated offsets may deserve further investigation but do not establish a cause.

Interpretation

MATCH means the two fields agree. MISMATCH means the weekday differs. INVALID means a date or weekday cannot be safely interpreted.

A matching weekday does not prove the intended event date is correct. The same weekday repeats every seven days.

The modular structure

The inherited ALBADORA weekday-code approach is formalized here as GCF. It separates century, year, month, and day components. The study's main application is a batch date–weekday consistency-checking workflow, not a claim to a historically unprecedented weekday equation.

W ≡ C + Y + M + D (mod 7)
CCentury residue
YWithin-century year residue
MMonth contribution
DDay of month

With c = ⌊year/100⌋ and y = year mod 100: C = [7,5,3,1][c mod 4] and Y = (y + ⌊y/4⌋) mod 7. The Gregorian leap-year rule selects the month codes below.

Cached calculation

B(r,m) ≡ C + Y + M (mod 7)
W ≡ B(r,m) + D (mod 7)

GCF-400 stores 4,800 reusable combined contributions (400 year positions × 12 months) in a Uint8Array. They are prepared once per page load; C, Y and M are not recomputed for every imported date.

Explaining a discrepancy

R ≡ Wrecorded − WGCF (mod 7)

Zero means agreement; a nonzero residue means disagreement. A signed value from −3 to +3 is displayed for reviewers. A residue cannot identify which source field is wrong.

The Gregorian 400-year calendar comprises 146,097 days, exactly 20,871 weeks. Precomputing that repeating pattern is an implementation optimization, not a new weekday theorem. Established algorithms and their formal comparison belong in the research manuscript, not this user-facing validator.
